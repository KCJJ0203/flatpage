Device check

Test A — camera round-trip. Tap Shoot, take any photo, come back. Repeat 3 times. If the counter resets to 0 or the "page loaded" line reappears, the page was reloaded and IndexedDB restore is mandatory.

Test B — share a PDF. Tap Share. Success means the iOS share sheet opens and "Save to Files" produces a readable one-page PDF.

Test C — memory. Tap Load big 5 times. If the tab crashes, the full-resolution hold strategy is too aggressive.
